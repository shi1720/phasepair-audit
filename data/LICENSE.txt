Derived area profiles, public cohort identifiers and example contour geometry derive from the Vestibular-Schwannoma-SEG dataset, March 2021 contour export.

Source dataset: https://doi.org/10.7937/TCIA.9YTJ-5Q73
Source paper: Shapey J, Kujawa A, Dorent R, et al. Segmentation of vestibular schwannoma from MRI, an open annotated dataset and baseline algorithm. Scientific Data 8, 286 (2021). https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-021-01064-w
